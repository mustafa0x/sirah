stage: preparations

## why

Trust and careful planning sit side by side: the Prophet ﷺ relied on Allah and still prepared every step.

## moment: visit
title: The noon visit

At the height of noon, an hour at which he never used to come, the Prophet ﷺ arrived at Abu Bakr’s house with his face covered. He said he had been given permission to leave. Abu Bakr asked to go with him, and he said yes.

### young

One hot noon, the Prophet ﷺ came to Abu Bakr’s house with big news: it was time to go. Abu Bakr asked to come too, and he said yes.

## note: E02
reference: Sahih al-Bukhari 3905

> فجهزناهما أحث الجهاز، وصنعنا لهما سفرة في جراب، فقطعت أسماء بنت أبي بكر قطعة من نطاقها، فربطت به على فم الجراب

The selected passage describes provisions and Asma bint Abi Bakr using part of her waist-belt to tie the bag.

## note: E06
reference: Sahih al-Bukhari 3905

> واستأجر رسول الله ﷺ وأبو بكر رجلًا من بني الديل ... هاديًا خريتًا ... فدفعا إليه راحلتيهما، وواعداه غار ثور بعد ثلاث ليال

The selected passage describes hiring a skilled guide, entrusting him with two riding animals, and arranging the meeting at Thawr.
