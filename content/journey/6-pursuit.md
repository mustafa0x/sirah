# A rider on the road

stage: pursuit

## N06a
sources: E13

Quraysh had offered a reward for each of the two travellers. Suraqah ibn Malik recounts that a man came to his gathering and said he had just seen figures near the coast. Suraqah recognised who they must be, and set out after them.

### young

Quraysh offered a big reward to catch them. A rider called Suraqah raced after them on his horse.

## N06b
sources: E14

By his own account, he came close enough to hear the Prophet ﷺ reciting. Then the forelegs of his horse sank into the ground up to the knees, and he was thrown from it.

### young

As he came close, his horse’s legs sank into the ground, and he fell off!

## N06c
sources: E15

He told them what Quraysh intended and offered them provisions. They took nothing from him and asked only that he keep their news hidden. At his request, ‘Amir ibn Fuhayrah wrote him a guarantee of safety on a piece of leather. Then they went on.

### young

Suraqah gave up the chase. They asked him only to keep their secret, and went on their way.

## why

A man who set out to capture them left asking for their protection. The story is full of reversals like this.
