# Three nights at Thawr

stage: thawr

## N03a
sources: E03

After the preparations, the Prophet ﷺ and Abu Bakr sheltered in a cave on Mount Thawr. ‘Aishah’s account in Sahih al-Bukhari records that they stayed there for three nights. This was a stage within the journey, not its destination.

### young

The Prophet ﷺ and Abu Bakr hid in a cave on Mount Thawr for three nights.

## N03b
sources: E04

During the stay, ‘Abdullah ibn Abi Bakr brought news of plans against them. He came after darkness and left before daybreak, returning to Makkah. His contribution was information.

### young

Each night, Abu Bakr’s son ‘Abdullah came in the dark to tell them the news from Makkah.

## N03c
sources: E05

‘Amir ibn Fuhayrah provided another kind of help. He tended sheep and brought them near after nightfall, making milk available. The account describes this on each of the three nights.

### young

A shepherd called ‘Amir brought his sheep close every evening, so that they had milk to drink.

## N03d
sources: E08

Abu Bakr’s own account brings us closer to the danger of discovery. He was concerned that they might be seen if someone looked down. The Prophet reassured him.

### young

Abu Bakr was worried: if someone looked down, they would be seen! The Prophet ﷺ calmed him.

## N03e
sources: E09, E10

The Quran also recalls the two in the cave and the reassurance given to the companion. Ibn Kathir’s commentary on the passage explains the reassurance through Allah’s help and support.

### young

The Quran remembers what the Prophet ﷺ said to his friend in the cave: do not be sad, Allah is with us.

## why

In the most frightening moment, the lesson is calm trust: Allah is with us.
