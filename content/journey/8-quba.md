# Quba: the first stop

stage: quba

## N05a
sources: E11

In Madinah, Muslims went out to wait after hearing that the Prophet ﷺ had left Makkah. The arrival account describes their reception of him and his stay among Bani ‘Amr ibn ‘Awf.

### young

In Madinah, Muslims went out to wait for the Prophet ﷺ. The arrival account tells how they welcomed him.

## N07a
sources: E16

According to ‘Urwa, he stayed among them for a few more than ten nights. There he founded the mosque that the account calls the one founded on piety, and he prayed in it.

### young

They first stopped at Quba, near Madinah, where a mosque was founded.

## why

A mosque was founded at Quba, making worship a part of life in the new home.
