stage: departure

## why

His honesty held even then: on the night Quraysh plotted his death, he left ‘Ali to return all that Makkah had entrusted to him.

## moment: wealth
title: Stones under a cloth

Abu Bakr took all his money, five or six thousand dirhams. When his blind father came, fearing it gone, Asma laid stones in its niche under a cloth and let him feel them.

### young

Abu Bakr took all his money. To calm her blind grandfather, Asma let him feel stones under a cloth.

## moment: south
title: South, not north

Madinah lay to the north, but that was not the road they took. Under cover of night they turned the opposite way, south on the road towards Yemen, and went about five miles to Mount Thawr.

### young

Madinah was to the north, but they went south first, to a mountain called Thawr.

## event: leave
title: Leaving Makkah
when: Night of 27 Safar · 12–13 September 622

He had stayed on in Makkah through Muharram and Safar. The exact night was worked out by a modern biographer.

## note: E18
reference: Ibn Kathir, al-Bidayah wa al-Nihayah

> فاجتمعوا له في دار الندوة، وهي دار قصي بن كلاب التي كانت قريش لا تقضي أمرا إلا فيها، يتشاورون فيما يصنعون في أمر رسول الله ﷺ حين خافوه

Ibn Ishaq tells how Quraysh, now afraid of him, met in Dar al-Nadwah, the house where they settled all their affairs.

## note: E19
reference: Ibn Kathir, al-Bidayah wa al-Nihayah

> فلما رأى رسول الله ﷺ مكانهم قال لعلي بن أبي طالب: نم على فراشي وتسج ببردي هذا الحضرمي الأخضر، فنم فيه فإنه لن يخلص إليك شئ تكرهه منهم

Seeing the men at his door, the Prophet ﷺ tells ‘Ali to sleep in his bed under his green cloak, and promises him safety.

## note: E20
reference: Ibn Kathir, al-Bidayah wa al-Nihayah

> أما علي فإن رسول الله ﷺ أمره أن يتخلف حتى يؤدي عن رسول الله ﷺ الودائع التي كانت عنده للناس، وكان رسول الله ﷺ، وليس بمكة أحد عنده شئ يخشى عليه إلا وضعه عنده، لما يعلم من صدقه وأمانته

Ibn Ishaq tells why ‘Ali stayed behind: anyone in Makkah who feared for something precious had left it with the Prophet ﷺ.

## note: E23
reference: Ibn Kathir, al-Bidayah wa al-Nihayah

> احتمل أبو بكر ماله كله معه، خمسة آلاف درهم أو ستة آلاف درهم، فانطلق بها معه ... وأخذت أحجارا فوضعتها في كوة في البيت الذي كان أبي يضع ماله فيها، ثم وضعت عليها ثوبا

Asma recalls her father taking all his money, and the stones she laid where it had been kept, under a cloth, to calm her grandfather.

## note: E24
reference: Al-Rahiq al-Makhtum

> فقد سلك الطريق الذي يضاده تماما، وهو الطريق الواقع جنوب مكة، والمتجه نحو اليمن. سلك هذا الطريق نحو خمسة أميال، حتى بلغ إلى جبل يعرف بجبل ثور

Al-Mubarakfuri notes that he took the very opposite road from the one expected, south of Makkah towards Yemen.

## note: E34
reference: Al-Rahiq al-Makhtum

> غادر رسول الله ﷺ بيته في ليلة ٢٧ من شهر صفر سنة ١٤ من النبوة الموافق ١٢/ ١٣ سبتمبر سنة ٦٢٢ م

Al-Mubarakfuri dates the night he left his house: 27 Safar, in the fourteenth year of his prophethood.
