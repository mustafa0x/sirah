# Glossary

## pbuh
word: ﷺ
matches: ﷺ

Short for “peace and blessings of Allah be upon him”, said by Muslims after the Prophet’s name.

## hijrah
word: Hijrah
matches: Hijrah | الهجرة

The migration of the Prophet ﷺ and his companions from Makkah to Madinah, in 622.

## quraysh
word: Quraysh
matches: Quraysh | قريش

The leading tribe of Makkah. The Prophet ﷺ himself belonged to it.

## makkah
word: Makkah
matches: Makkah | مكة

The city where the Prophet ﷺ was born, home of the Ka‘bah.

## madinah
word: Madinah
matches: Madinah | المدينة

Then called Yathrib: the city that welcomed the Prophet ﷺ. Its name means “the city”.

## abubakr
word: Abu Bakr
matches: Abu Bakr | أبو بكر | أبي بكر

The Prophet’s closest friend and companion, who later led the Muslims after him.

## allah
word: Allah
matches: Allah | الله

The Arabic word for God.

## quran
word: Quran
matches: Quran | القرآن

The holy book of Islam, which Muslims believe is the word of Allah.

## bukhari
word: Sahih al-Bukhari
matches: al-Bukhari | البخاري

The collection of hadith that Muslims consider the most reliable.

## ibnkathir
word: Ibn Kathir
matches: Ibn Kathir | ابن كثير

A scholar of the fourteenth century whose history of the Prophet’s life is widely used.

## ibnishaq
word: Ibn Ishaq
matches: Ibn Ishaq | ابن إسحاق

An eighth-century historian whose accounts of the Prophet’s life are preserved by later writers.

## thawr
word: Thawr
matches: Thawr | ثور

A mountain south of Makkah, with a small cave near its top.

## quba
word: Quba
matches: Quba | قباء

A village on the edge of Madinah, where the Prophet ﷺ stopped first.

## ansar
word: Ansar
matches: Ansar | Helpers | الأنصار

"The Helpers": the Muslims of Madinah who took in the Prophet ﷺ and the emigrants.

## emigrants
word: Emigrants
matches: Emigrants | المهاجرين | المهاجرون

The Muslims who left Makkah for Madinah, leaving homes and wealth behind.

## banu
word: Banu
matches: Banu | بني | بنو

"Sons of": a clan named after its ancestor, as in Banu al-Najjar.

## aws
word: Aws
matches: Aws | الأوس

One of the two Arab tribes of Madinah, long at war with the other, Khazraj.

## khazraj
word: Khazraj
matches: Khazraj | الخزرج

One of the two Arab tribes of Madinah, long at war with the other, Aws.

## yathrib
word: Yathrib
matches: Yathrib | يثرب

The old name of Madinah, before the Prophet ﷺ settled there.

## aqabah
word: al-‘Aqabah
matches: al-‘Aqabah | العقبة

A mountain pass near Mina, where the people of Madinah pledged themselves to the Prophet ﷺ.

## mina
word: Mina
matches: Mina | منى

A valley outside Makkah where pilgrims camp during the days of the pilgrimage.

## tashriq
word: days of Tashriq
matches: Tashriq | التشريق

The three days after the Feast of Sacrifice, which pilgrims spend at Mina.

## pilgrimage
word: Pilgrimage
matches: pilgrimage | الحج

The yearly gathering at Makkah and its valleys, already ancient before Islam.

## abyssinia
word: Abyssinia
matches: Abyssinia | الحبشة

The Christian kingdom across the Red Sea where early Muslims found refuge.

## daralnadwah
word: Dar al-Nadwah
matches: Dar al-Nadwah | دار الندوة

The council house of Quraysh in Makkah, where its leaders settled their affairs.

## jibril
word: Jibril
matches: Jibril | جبريل

The angel Gabriel, who brought revelation to the Prophet ﷺ.

## yasin
word: Surat Ya-Sin
matches: Ya-Sin | يس

The thirty-sixth chapter (surah) of the Quran.

## najd
word: Najd
matches: Najd | نجد

The high central plateau of Arabia, east of Makkah and Madinah.

## hadramawt
word: Hadramawt
matches: Hadramawt | حضرمي

A region of southern Arabia, known for its cloth.

## nitaqayn
word: Dhat al-Nitaqayn
matches: Dhat al-Nitaqayn | ذات النطاقين

"She of the two waistbands": the name Asma, Abu Bakr's daughter, earned on the night of the journey.

## khirrit
word: khirrit
matches: khirrit | خريت

An expert guide, who knows the desert's hidden ways.

## samur
word: samur
matches: samur | السمر

A thorny desert acacia; its leaves are fodder for camels.

## freedman
word: Freedman
matches: freedman | مولى

A former slave, set free, who remains attached to the family that freed him.

## arrows
word: Divining arrows
matches: divining arrows | الأزلام

Marked arrows drawn before Islam to decide whether to act; Islam forbade them.

## sakinah
word: Tranquility
matches: tranquility | السكينة

The calm that Allah sends down on the believers' hearts.

## jinn
word: Jinn
matches: jinn | الجن

Beings created from fire, unseen by people.

## harrah
word: harrah
matches: harrah | الحرة

A field of black volcanic rock; Madinah lies between two of them.

## friday
word: Friday prayer
matches: Friday prayer | الجمعة

The congregational prayer at midday on Friday, with a sermon.

## rabialawwal
word: Rabi‘ al-Awwal
matches: Rabi‘ al-Awwal | ربيع الأول

The third month of the Arab calendar, in which the Prophet ﷺ reached Madinah.

## safar
word: Safar
matches: Safar | صفر

The second month of the Arab calendar, in which the Prophet ﷺ left Makkah.

## muharram
word: Muharram
matches: Muharram | المحرم

The first month of the Arab calendar.

## dhualhijjah
word: Dhu al-Hijjah
matches: Dhu al-Hijjah | ذي الحجة

The last month of the Arab calendar, the month of the pilgrimage.

## mirbad
word: mirbad
matches: mirbad | مربد

An open yard where dates are spread out to dry.

## labin
word: labin
matches: labin

Bricks of clay, dried in the sun rather than fired.

## rajaz
word: rajaz
matches: rajaz | الرجز

Short rhythmic verse, chanted while working or travelling.

## qibla
word: qibla
matches: qibla | القبلة

The direction Muslims face in prayer: first Jerusalem, later the Ka‘bah.

## kabah
word: Ka‘bah
matches: Ka‘bah | الكعبة

The cube-shaped House of Allah in Makkah, which Muslims face in prayer.

## saamudd
word: sa‘ and mudd
matches: mudd | الصاع | المد

Measures of grain: a mudd is a double handful, and four of them make a sa‘.

## idhkhir
word: idhkhir
matches: idhkhir | إذخر

A fragrant grass of the valleys around Makkah.

## badr
word: Badr
matches: Badr | بدر

The first great battle between the Muslims and Quraysh, two years after the Hijrah.

## siffin
word: Siffin
matches: Siffin | صفين

A battle between Muslims, decades later, in the time of ‘Ali.

## hadith
word: Hadith
matches: hadith | الحديث

A report of what the Prophet ﷺ said or did, passed on by named narrators.

## hasan
word: hasan
matches: hasan

"Good": a report graded reliable, a step below the soundest (sahih).

## mursal
word: mursal
matches: mursal | مرسل

A report from someone who did not witness it and does not name who told him.

## munkar
word: munkar
matches: munkar | منكر

"Rejected": a report graded unreliable, often because it contradicts stronger ones.

## salman
word: Salman al-Farisi
matches: Salman | سلمان

A Persian who searched for the true religion and became a companion of the Prophet ﷺ.
