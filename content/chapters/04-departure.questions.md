# Questions: Departure from Makkah

## hch_departure_q1
level: beginner
scope: lesson
answer: a
sources: `sp_departure_chapter_wait:p1–p4`

Why did the Prophet ﷺ remain in Mecca after his companions, according to Ibn Ishaq?

### a

To await permission to emigrate

> Ibn Ishaq states that he remained in Mecca awaiting permission to emigrate.

### b

Because he had already reached Quba

> Quba appears in the later report of Ali joining him, not as a reason for the Prophet ﷺ to remain in Mecca.

### why

Ibn Ishaq relates that he was waiting for permission, while Ali and Abu Bakr remained in Mecca after the other emigrants.

## hch_departure_q2

Which plan did the Quraysh council settle on in the account from Ibn Abbas?

### a

Postponing the matter until another season

> The report does not mention postponement until a season; it ends with a plan to kill him involving the tribes.

### b

Young men from the tribes joining in the killing

> After proposals to imprison and exile him, Abu Jahl proposes that young men from the tribes strike him together in a single blow.

### why

Abu Jahl links the tribes' joint action to the dispersal of responsibility for bloodshed among them; the text reports the Najdi elder's approval and the men's parting in agreement on this plan.

What exception does al-Suwayani mention in his assessment of the report about the Najdi shaykh?

Mention al-Suwayani's exception to his assessment

> Al-Suwayani explicitly excludes the mention of Iblis from his favorable assessment of the report.

### b

Assert that all critics authenticated it

> Al-Suwayani's exception for Iblis contradicts extending his authentication to every detail and every critic.

### why

## hch_departure_q4
level: intermediate
scope: source_study
answer: b

Which chain does Ibn Ishaq explicitly give for the account of scattering dust?

### a

Ibn Ishaq ← Ibn Abbas ← Yazid ibn Ziyad

> This choice conflates the two routes and attributes the account to Ibn Abbas; here Ibn Ishaq begins a different chain through Yazid ibn Ziyad from Muhammad ibn Kab.

### b

Ibn Ishaq ← Yazid ibn Ziyad ← Muhammad ibn Kab al-Qurazi

> Ibn Ishaq's account states: “Yazid ibn Ziyad told me, from Muhammad ibn Kab al-Qurazi, who said:”.

### why

The chain for the dust account begins with Ibn Ishaq saying that Yazid ibn Ziyad told him on the authority of Muhammad ibn Kab al-Qurazi; it is not a continuation of the route from Ibn Abbas.

What does Urwa's brief account say about Ali?

### a

He lay on the Prophet's ﷺ bed

> Urwa mentions Ali's lying on the Prophet's ﷺ bed at the end of his brief account.

### b

Abu Jahl's dialogue with everyone present at the council

> The detail of Abu Jahl's words at the door belongs to the report from Muhammad ibn Kab, not Urwa's shorter selection.

### why

Urwa's account brings together the forms of the plot, departure toward the cave, and Ali's lying on the bed, without Abu Jahl's detailed dialogue.

## hch_departure_q6
level: intermediate
scope: source_study
answer: b
sources: `sp_departure_chapter_trust:p1`, `sp_departure_chapter_arrival:p1`

What qualification did Ibn Ishaq explicitly give in the report about the entrusted property?

### a

A named chain of direct hearing in the same passage

> Had Ibn Ishaq named a transmitter for the entrusted property here, he would not have said that the report reached him in this form.

### b

“As it reached me” (fima balaghani)

> The text repeats “as it reached me” (fima balaghani) when mentioning that Ali was told of the departure and instructed to return the entrusted property.

### why

The preserved formula “as it reached me” (fima balaghani) is an unsourced report from Ibn Ishaq; it must not be converted into the name of an omitted transmitter.

When does the account of the entrusted property say that Ali joined him?

### a

After returning the entrusted property

> The text links Ali's joining him to his completion of the task of returning the property for which he had stayed behind.

### b

Before sleeping on the bed

> This text does not place Ali's joining him before the night of the bed; rather, it mentions his remaining in Mecca for three nights and their days.

### why

The report says Ali finished returning the entrusted property and then joined the Prophet ﷺ and lodged with him at the home of Kulthum ibn Hidm.

What condition did al-Suwayani place on his assessment of the route through Abu Balj?

### a

Rejecting all reports about Ali without exception

> The discussion of the route through Abu Balj does not invalidate every report about Ali or all their wordings.

### b

That he not be alone in transmitting contradictory wording

> Al-Suwayani's phrase “there is no problem with it” is qualified by the condition that Abu Balj not be alone in transmitting contradictory wording.

### why

Al-Suwayani cites both endorsements and criticism of Abu Balj, then qualifies his assessment of the chain by the absence of a wording that Abu Balj transmits alone in contradiction to others.

Where does the address expressing love for Mecca appear in this chapter?

### a

In the al-Hazwara report from Ibn Adi ibn al-Hamra

How did al-Bayhaqi describe certain other narrations in the context of al-Hazwara?

### a

As specifying the hour of an encounter at the bed

> Al-Bayhaqi identifies errors in the mentioned routes, not the hour of an encounter at the bed.

### b

As mistaken, while preferring the group's version

> Al-Bayhaqi mentions an error by Mamar and the route through Muhammad ibn Amr, then prefers the group's version.

### why

Al-Bayhaqi calls the two routes he names mistaken and prefers the group's version; he does not apply this judgment to every departure account.
